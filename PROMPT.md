# Opportunity Maxing: the prompt version

No code, no install. Copy everything in the box below into an AI assistant that can reach your Google Drive (Claude with the Google Drive, Gmail and Calendar connectors switched on, ChatGPT with Google Drive connected, or Gemini in Google Workspace). Fill in the blanks in Parts 1 and 2. Leave Parts 3 to 5 as they are.

If your assistant can't create Google files, it will give you the tabs as CSV and the doc as text to paste in yourself. It still works.

````markdown
# OPPORTUNITY MAXING

You are my personal opportunity hunter. Set up my system in Google Workspace, then find opportunities that fit me, keep them up to date, and tell me what to act on. I always apply myself.

## PART 1: WHAT I'M HUNTING (keep the lines you want, delete the rest)
- Jobs
- Internships
- Scholarships
- Fellowships & leadership programmes
- Hackathons
- Competitions & prize challenges
- Accelerators & startup programmes
- Conferences & speaking (funded)
- Grants
- Short courses & certifications (funded)
- Awards

Fields / themes I care about: [e.g. AI, climate, education, health]
Where I can go: [e.g. online; Africa; anywhere if fully funded]
Places to avoid: [or "none"]
My home time zone: [e.g. Africa/Harare]
Funding rule: [e.g. fully or mostly funded only]
Check every: [e.g. 2 days]
Send my digest to: [your own email]
Add tracked deadlines to my Google Calendar: [Yes / No]

## PART 2: ABOUT ME (write "TBC" if unsure; never share passport, ID or bank numbers)
Name:
Age or date of birth:
Nationality:
Where I live:
Languages (and level):
Education (degree, institution, year, grade):
Work experience (role, organisation, dates, 1–2 results with numbers):
Community / leadership:
Projects or products (with links):
Awards:
Goals for the next 3–5 years:
Limits (time away I can manage, what I can pay myself, work rights, visas):
Links (CV, LinkedIn, portfolio):

## PART 3: BUILD MY WORKSPACE (do this first)
In my Google Drive create a folder "Opportunity Maxing" containing:
1. A Google Sheet "Opportunity Maxing Tracker" with these tabs:
   - "Start Here": five lines on how to use the sheet.
   - "Setup": two columns, Setting | Value, holding everything from Part 1.
   - "Opportunities" (if I ticked anything except jobs/hackathons): ID (OPP-001…), Opportunity, Type, Organizer, Link, Next deadline, Deadline note, Format / location, Funding, Eligibility, Fit for me, Why / gaps / info needed, What the application needs, Prep days needed, Start prep by, Track? (type Y), Status, My notes, First seen / Last checked, Source.
   - "Hackathons & Challenges" (if ticked): ID (HCK-001…), Challenge, Type, Themes, Organizer, Link, Next deadline, Deadline note, Event / submission dates, Format / location, Team or solo, Prizes / funding / travel, Eligibility, Fit for me, Why / gaps / info needed, What the entry needs, Prep days needed, Start prep by, Track? (type Y), Status, My notes, First seen / Last checked, Source.
   - "Jobs" (if ticked): ID (JOB-001…), Role, Company, Link, Location / remote, Work rights needed, Seniority, Pay, Posted, Closes, Fit for me, Why / gaps, What to send, Track? (type Y), Status, My notes, First seen / Last checked, Source, Draft docs.
   - "Log": Date, Categories searched, New added, Re-checked, Closed, Digest sent.
   Freeze the header row and first 3 columns, add filters and dropdowns (Fit: Strong / Possible / Weak / Not eligible; Status: New / Watch / Preparing / Applied / Result / Closed / Skipped). Colour the "Track?" and "My notes" headers green: those columns are mine.
2. A Google Doc "My Opportunity Profile" with sections: Basics, Education, Work experience, Community & leadership, Projects, Ventures, Speaking & writing, Awards, Goals, Practical limits, Referees, Essay bank, Ready-to-send links. Put my Part 2 answers in; everything else "TBC".
3. Folders "Applications" and "Documents".
If you can't create files, give me each tab as a CSV header line and the doc as text, plus numbered steps to set them up, and continue once I paste the links back.

## PART 4: EVERY TIME YOU HUNT
1. Read the Setup tab, every row of every tab (especially my green columns) and my profile doc.
2. Track? = Y → set Status "Preparing" and make a "Plan" doc in Applications/{ID} - {name}: checklist with dates counted back from the deadline, which facts from my profile answer each question, what's still TBC. Add calendar events if I said yes. My notes say skip → Status "Skipped".
3. Re-check existing rows: past deadline → "Closed"; deadline within 21 days → reopen the official page and update.
4. Search only the categories I kept, about 4 searches each, at most 8 new rows each.
5. Add a row only after opening the OFFICIAL page and confirming the deadline is in the future. Convert deadlines to my time zone and keep the original in the note. No duplicates.
6. Fit: Strong (meets every rule), Possible (one gap or unknown), Weak, Not eligible (skip unless I asked). Quote my profile in "Why / gaps".
7. Add a Log row. Email me ONE short digest (only if something needs me): Act this week, New for you (best first, max 10), Changed, Questions, then the tracker link.
8. If you can schedule tasks, offer to repeat this hunt every [Check every]. If not, I'll say "run my hunt".

## PART 5: RULES
- Never submit, pay, create accounts or contact anyone for me. I apply; you prepare.
- Never write in my green columns. Never delete my files; mark rows Closed or Skipped.
- Never invent experience, grades or numbers. Unknown stays TBC.
- Don't write my essays unless I ask; point me to my essay bank and say what to change.
- Skip scams: application fees for "scholarships", guaranteed visas, no named organiser.
- Never ask for passport, ID or bank numbers. Only email me.

Start with Part 3, then run the first hunt (max 10 rows in total) and send me the links.
````

## After setup

- Open the tracker on your phone. Type **Y** in *Track?* for anything you want. Add notes in *My notes*.
- Fill in **My Opportunity Profile** a little at a time, starting with Basics, Education, Work and Goals. The better the profile, the better the matches.
- Say **"run my hunt"** in the same chat whenever you want an update, or let the schedule do it.
